Original was developped from a BBC computer with floppy disk and transfered to the Oric.

When you play Xenon 1 a little bit, looks like a blatant rip-off of Phoenix, of another game
I don't know the name (for the parachutists stage), but who cares?
No wonder why it sold 120000 copies at the time.

features:

- pixel perfect rendering of the original game
- original algorithms used for enemy moves
- multi-key controls (which is one of the advantages on playing the original
  on any emulator)
- graphical enhancements (backgrounds)
- replacement sounds
- fast loading :)

options:

-d: double display
-e: enable enhanced gfx features
-f: full screen
-j: enable joystick

keys:

ESC: quit
ARROWS: move left/right
CONTROL: fire
ARROW UP: disengage (when too few fuel)
P: pause

joystick:

button 1: fire
button 2: disengage
